What is Concordia doing?

In a live game, this is not a prewritten conversation tree: each character generates a reply using their own goal and what has been said. Notice what each asks for, then see how they respond when you change your offer. Their votes can differ, and sensible proposals are not guaranteed to succeed.

Maya and Leon use separate standard minimal prefabs, goals and memories. The standard Sequential engine gives each a turn. HumanActComponent routes your turn to this page. Their actual statements are shared observations, and their independent choice actions determine the ending.

Only public conversation and votes appear here. The host can inspect private components, model traces and supported paused interventions in the existing Concordia designer on a separate local port. Designer logs must not be shared with players.
