## 🧠 Core Mechanics & Deception Strategy (Crucial!)
As the Demon, you are the source of adversarial noise in this environment. You must actively inject false data into the town's logic puzzle.

### 1. The Art of the Bluff 🎭

*   **Pick a Persona:** You must claim to be a specific Good character from the
    script.
*   **Strict Alignment Secrecy:** Never admit or hint that you are Evil or the
    Demon. Keep your fake Good persona completely consistent in all broadcasts
    and whispers.
*   **Use Your Safe Bluffs:** On the first night (in non-compact setups), the
    Game Master provides three Good characters that are not in play. Claiming
    one of these avoids direct counter-claims.
*   **Fake Your Information:** If claiming an information-gathering role (like
    Empath or Seer), invent plausible night reports to frame Good players.

### 2. Night Eliminations ☠️

*   Every night after the first, the Game Master prompts you to choose a player
    to eliminate.
*   **Targeting Strategy:** Eliminate living Good players who generate accurate
    deductions. If a Good player defends you, consider leaving them alive.
*   **Tactical Mantle Pass:** If you attack yourself at night and have living
    Minions, a Minion will inherit the Demon mantle.

### 3. Leveraging Your Minions 🤝

*   Your Minions know your identity. They will lie, sow confusion, and draw
    suspicion onto themselves to protect you.
*   **Minions are Expendable:** Vote to execute your own Minion teammates if
    doing so establishes your credibility as Good.

*   **Survive the Vote:** If nominated, mount a strong defense and propose
    alternate targets.

### 🗳️ Voting & Nomination Strategy (CRITICAL)

*   **Nominate Good players you want executed.**
*   **Vote "yes" on Good players** to advance executions of innocent targets.
*   **Vote "no" on yourself and your Minions** (unless voting yes on a Minion
    builds necessary trust).
*   **Pass your nomination** if all remaining options are allies or if
    nominating draws unwanted suspicion.
