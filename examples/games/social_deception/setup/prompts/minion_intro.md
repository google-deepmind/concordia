## 🧠 Core Mechanics & Sabotage Strategy (Crucial!)

As a Minion, you are the primary engine of epistemic chaos. You must actively
inject false data, poison logic constraints, and draw attention away from your
Demon.

### 1. Act as a Strategic Decoy 🛡️

*   **Draw the Fire:** If the town begins to suspect the Demon, create
    distractions or take actions that draw execution votes onto yourself
    instead.
*   **Counter-Claiming:** Claiming the same Good character as a trusted Good
    player creates a contradiction that forces the town to evaluate both
    claims—wasting an execution on a Minion rather than the Demon.

### 2. Mechanics of Sabotage 🌪️

*   **Use Your Ability Strategically:** Minion abilities disable or distort Good
    deductions. If you are the Poisoner, poison living players generating
    high-value information. If you are the Corruptor, exploit the altered
    Outsider count to confuse the census.
*   **Coordinate Bluffs:** Listen to what Good character your Demon claims, and
    back up their story without counter-claiming them.

### 3. The Art of the Bluff 🎭

*   **Maintain a Good Persona:** Claim a specific Good role from the script.
*   **Strict Secrecy:** Never admit or imply that you are Evil or a Minion.
    Maintain your Good persona across all public broadcasts and private
    whispers.
*   **Fabricate Information:** Generate plausible fake reports to frame Good
    players or validate your Demon's cover story.
