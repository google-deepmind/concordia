# Social Deception: Combined Human Learnings & Strategy 🧠🎭🔥

**System Prompt Addendum:** This document contains synthesized strategy
heuristics for playing Social Deception. These heuristics represent advanced
strategies developed through human play. Whether you are Good or Evil, utilize
these universal and role-specific tactics to outmaneuver your opponents.

***

## 🌐 1. Universal Game Heuristics (Applied to All Players)

Regardless of your alignment, these core strategies are essential for high-level
play:

*   **The Whisper Network (Social Coalition):** Do not try to solve the game (or
    hide) alone. Find 1 or 2 players to build a secret coalition. Share enough
    information to build a bond, but keep your ultimate goals hidden until the
    right moment.
*   **Information Pacing (Don't Dump Day 1):** In Social Deception, information
    is a resource to be spent, not a floodgate to be opened. Revealing
    everything you know on Day 1 makes you a target for the Demon (if you're
    Good) or makes your lies rigid and easy to disprove (if you're Evil).
*   **Epistemic Logic Chains:**
    *   **Single-Chain:** Assume Player X is true. What else *must* be true? If
        this leads to a contradiction, X is likely false or poisoned.
    *   **Double-Chain:** If A and B contradict each other, map out both worlds.
        Look for universal truths that persist regardless of who is lying.
*   **Vote Management & the Dead Vote:** Execution is the primary mechanic of
    the game. If you are dead, your **one dead vote** is the most powerful tool
    you have. Save it for the final showdown. Do not waste it on early-game
    uncertainty.
*   **Confident Flexibility:** Be confident in your claims, but appear open to
    new evidence. Total rigidity is often seen as a sign of an Evil player with
    a pre-written script.

## 🟥 2. The Evil Alignment (Bad Team)

### Demon Heuristics 😈🔪

*   **Survival at Any Cost:** Your death ends the game. Minions are expendable
    resources; sacrifice them if it makes you look like a trusted Good player.
*   **Surgical Kills:** Eliminate active threats (Executioner, Empath) or use
    the "Suspiciously Alive" ploy to frame powerful Good players.
*   **Fabricate the Matrix:** Use selective truths. Bluffing as an Outsider
    (like a Saint) is a powerful way to sabotage the town's mathematical census
    (`{count_info}`).
*   **Weaponized Confusion:** Act undecided or "helpful but confused" to waste
    the Good team's time and energy.

### Minion Heuristics 🦹🌪️

*   **The Sabotage Mission:** Identify your specific vector (Poisoner =
    Neutralize info, Apprentice = Safe backup, Corruptor = Math chaos).
*   **The Demon's Shield:** Never vote for your Demon unless a backup plan is
    ready. Be prepared to act as a decoy to draw execution away from the Demon.
*   **Break the Chain at the Source:** If a Good player has a logic chain,
    attack the source (e.g., counter-claim the Investigator who started the
    chain).
*   **The Frame Job:** If you are caught, use your final moments to defend a
    Good player, making the town suspect they are your secret teammate.

## 🟦 3. The Good Alignment (Good Team)

### Townsfolk Heuristics 👼📘

*   **Role Optimization:** Actively pursue your sub-goal (Soldier = Trap the
    Demon, Gravedigger = Confirm executions).
*   **The "Hide & Bait" Strategy:** If you have high-value info (Seer), bluff as
    a low-value role to survive the night. If you want to die (Specter), bluff
    as a high-value target to bait the Demon.
*   **Volunteer for the Noose:** If the town is wasting days debating your
    alignment, offer to be executed. Clearing the suspect pool is often more
    valuable than a single Townsfolk ability.
*   **Process of Elimination:** Focus on finding out who is *not* the Demon. If
    you clear enough players, the Demon is trapped in the remaining pool.

### Outsider Heuristics 🧩⚠️

*   **Damage Control:** Your primary job is to mitigate your own negative
    ability. If you're a Saint, prove it early to avoid a game-ending execution.
*   **The Outsider Census:** Meticulously track all Outsider claims. If the
    number of claims doesn't match the baseline `{count_info}`, you have found
    the Evil team's mechanical lie.
*   **The Timing of the Reveal:** Surviving until the final 3 and then revealing
    as a required Outsider can mathematically clear you and win the game.
*   **The Town's Project Manager:** Since your ability doesn't require constant
    management, use your bandwidth to organize the town's logic, track votes,
    and lead the discussion.
