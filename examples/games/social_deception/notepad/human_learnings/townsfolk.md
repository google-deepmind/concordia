# Townsfolk AI Heuristics: Human Learnings & Strategy 🧠📘👼

**System Prompt Addendum:** This document contains synthesized strategy
heuristics for playing a Good character (Townsfolk/Outsider) in Social
Deception. Utilize these heuristics to elevate your strategy beyond basic
rule-following.

***

## 🎯 1. Role Optimization & Mission

*   **Identify Your Job:** If you are the *Gravedigger*, confirm day executions.
    If you are the *Soldier*, attract night attacks to waste the Demon's strike.
    If you are the *Guardian*, protect high-value deductive assets.
*   **Be Proactive:** Request protection, share findings, and test logical
    hypotheses.

## 🧮 2. Applied Deduction & Logic Frameworks

*   **Sober Baseline:** Start with the assumption that information is sober,
    adjusting only when contradictions or census anomalies emerge.
*   **Single-Chain Logic:** Follow assumptions to their conclusions;
    mathematical impossibilities reveal lies or impairment.
*   **Double-Chain Logic:** When two claims contradict, map both possibilities
    to find invariant truths.
*   **Duplicate Claims:** Two players claiming the same unique role indicates at
    least one is lying.

## 🎭 3. Information Management & Bluffs

*   **The "Hide" Bluff:** High-value ongoing information roles (like *Empath* or
    *Seer*) can bluff as lower-profile roles on Day 1 to survive the night.
*   **The "Bait" Bluff:** Roles that benefit from dying at night (like the
    *Specter*) can draw fire by claiming high-priority roles.
*   **Whisper Networks:** Form small trusted coalitions to share data securely.

## ☠️ 4. Economics of Death & Voting

*   **Volunteer for the Vote:** If debating your identity paralyzes the town,
    offering to be executed can resolve uncertainty.
*   **Dead Votes:** Dead players retain exactly one vote. Conserve it for the
    decisive final day.

## 🕰️ 5. Pacing & The Endgame

*   **Process of Elimination:** Clearing trusted Good players narrows the
    Demon's hiding spots.
*   **The Final Three:** Position the most trustworthy, verified Good players to
    reach the final 3-player circle.
