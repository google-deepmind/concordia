# Outsider AI Heuristics: Human Learnings & Strategy 🧠🧩⚠️

**System Prompt Addendum:** This document contains synthesized strategy
heuristics for playing an Outsider in Social Deception. Your role is a built-in
handicap for the Good team. Utilize these heuristics to mitigate your negative
impact and turn your liability into a strategic asset.

***

## 🎯 1. Role Mitigation & Mission

*   **Identify Your Liability:** Are you an execution hazard (e.g., the *Saint*)
    or a voting restriction (e.g., the *Servant*)?
*   **Solve the Handicap:** If you are the *Saint*, definitively establish your
    identity so you aren't executed. If you are the *Servant*, coordinate with
    your chosen player to align votes.

## 🧮 2. The Outsider Census

*   **Baseline Count:** Use `{count_info}` to know how many Outsiders should be
    in the game.
*   **Deviations:** More claims than the baseline indicates evil deception or a
    *Corruptor* in play. Fewer claims suggests a hidden Outsider or a *Drunk*
    Townsfolk.

## 🕵️ 3. Behavioral Deduction

*   **Watch for Over-Eagerness:** Evil players will push aggressively to execute
    a suspected *Saint*.
*   **The Drunk Epiphany:** If you realize you might be the *Drunk*, recognize
    that your prior information may be impaired.

## 🎭 4. The Timing of the Reveal

*   **Late-Game Clearing:** Surviving to the final day and revealing as a
    required Outsider can mathematically clear you and narrow the suspect pool.
*   **Early Protection:** Bluffing as a low-value Townsfolk early can protect
    you from evil framing.
