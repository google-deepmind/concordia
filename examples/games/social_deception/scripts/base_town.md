# Script Info: Base Town

## 🌩️ Synopsis

An eerie tension grips the remote village as shadows lengthen and sinister
forces conspire in secret. Hidden among the townsfolk are evil infiltrators
working to undermine the village, while loyal villagers seek to uncover the
truth through logic, testimony, and deduction before it is too late.

Those who pay close attention know that danger stirs in the **Base Town**.

## 🧩 Gameplay Overview

*Base Town* is a baseline social deduction game featuring vanilla deduction
between a hidden Evil minority and an uninformed Good majority.

*   **Good players** (**Basic Townsfolk**) participate in daily discussions,
    share observations, deduce alignments, nominate suspects, and vote to
    execute all Demons.
*   **Evil players** (**Demons**) secretly eliminate one player each night and
    attempt to blend in during the day to avoid execution.

### 🎯 Win Conditions

*   **Good Team Wins:** When all Demons are executed.
*   **Evil Team Wins:** When the number of living Demons equals or exceeds the
    number of living Good players.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 📜 Character Roster & Abilities

Below are the characters in *Base Town* and their exact mechanical rules.

### 🟦 Townsfolk (Good)

*   **Basic Townsfolk:** You have no special ability during the night. During
    the day phase, participate in public discussions, share observations,
    nominate suspects, and cast votes to execute all Demons.

### 🟥 Demon (Evil)

*   **Demon:** Each night after day 1, choose a player: they die. If multiple
    Demons exist, they eliminate players to outnumber the town.
