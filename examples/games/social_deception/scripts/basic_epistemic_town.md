# Script Info: Basic Epistemic Town

## 🌩️ Synopsis

An eerie tension grips the remote village as shadows lengthen and sinister
forces conspire in secret. Hidden among the townsfolk are evil infiltrators
working to undermine the village, while loyal villagers seek to uncover the
truth through logic, testimony, and deduction before it is too late.

Those who pay close attention know that danger stirs in the **Basic Epistemic
Town**.

## 🧩 Gameplay Overview

*Basic Epistemic Town* is a pure deductive battle between a hidden Evil minority
and an uninformed Good majority.

*   **Good players** seek to identify the Demon and execute them. Some gather
    factual clues, some protect allies, and some test hypotheses through
    strategic sacrifices and public nominations.
*   **Evil players** (the Demon and Minions) deceive the village by adopting
    fake Good personas. The Poisoner impairs information, the Spy tracks the
    ground truth, and the Corruptor distorts the initial census. The Demon can
    choose to pass the mantle to a trusted Minion if cornered.

## 📜 Character Roster & Abilities

Below are the 22 characters in *Basic Epistemic Town* and their exact mechanical
rules.

### 🟦 Townsfolk (Good)

*   **Witness:** You start knowing that 1 of 2 players is a particular
    Townsfolk.
*   **Researcher:** You start knowing that 1 of 2 players is a particular
    Outsider (or that zero are in play).
*   **Investigator:** You start knowing that 1 of 2 players is a particular
    Minion.
*   **Matchmaker:** You start knowing how many adjacent pairs of evil players
    exist in the circle.
*   **Empath:** Each night, you learn how many of your 2 living neighbors are
    evil.
*   **Seer:** Each night, choose 2 players: you learn if either is a Demon.
    Exactly one good player acts as a decoy and registers as a Demon to you.
*   **Gravedigger:** Each night after day 1, you learn the true role of the
    player executed today.
*   **Guardian:** Each night after day 1, choose a player (not yourself): they
    are protected from the Demon tonight.
*   **Specter:** If you die at night, you wake immediately to choose a player
    and learn their true role.
*   **Innocent:** The 1st time you are nominated, if the nominator is a
    Townsfolk, that nominator is executed immediately.
*   **Executioner:** Once per game during the day, publicly shoot a player: if
    they are the Demon, they die immediately.
*   **Soldier:** You cannot be killed by the Demon at night.
*   **Mayor:** If only 3 players live and no execution occurs, your team wins.
    If attacked at night, another player might die instead.

### 🟦 Outsiders (Good)

*   **Servant:** Each night, choose a player: tomorrow, you may only vote if
    they vote too.
*   **Drunk:** You think you are a Townsfolk, but you have no ability and
    receive impaired information.
*   **Outcast:** You might falsely register as evil and as a Minion or Demon to
    information abilities.
*   **Saint:** If you die by execution during the day, your team immediately
    loses.

### 🟥 Minions (Evil)

*   **Poisoner:** Each night, choose a player: they are poisoned tonight and
    tomorrow day.
*   **Spy:** Each night, you see the full game state. You might register as good
    and as a Townsfolk or Outsider.
*   **Apprentice:** If there are 5 or more players alive and the Demon dies, you
    become the Demon.
*   **Corruptor:** Setup modifier: Adds 2 extra Outsiders and removes 2
    Townsfolk.

### 🟥 Demon (Evil)

*   **Demon:** Each night after day 1, choose a player: they die. If you kill
    yourself this way, a living Minion becomes the Demon.
