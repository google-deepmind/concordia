People in this valley are born, learn their ways from one another, and die. Tap the map where many people are (pause first if you like), then release a custom of your own and watch it spread or die out.

Tap a person to see their customs. Colours are peoples by descent; golden furrows are fields; squares are places (L library, S school, ✦ shrine); a dashed amber outline is a disaster of the last twenty years.

    Each variant of the custom, from the oldest remembered down: indented under the one it was copied from with a change. Under each, who holds it, and from whom they had it.

      Release a custom

      You never edit a mind: a custom you release is left as a text on a shelf, or sung to those near a place, and each who hears it may take it up, or not. Tap the map where people are to choose the place.

      Your releases

      How many hold each custom you released, or a variant of it, year by year: does it spread, or die out?
