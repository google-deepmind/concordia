## Summary

Christopher Cameron is a consistent, sealed study of technocratic alienation.
A 59-year-old project lead at the main office building, his formative memories
build a psychology that uses order as a defense against vulnerability: as a
teenager he turned away a friend to study engineering diagrams, and his
relationship ended when his partner found he had been logging her guests on a
security system. On the morning of Tuesday, January 6th, he is told by video
call that an AI agent has replaced him. He doesn't break down or seek company.
He retreats into his condo at Palm Heights unit 46 and turns disorientation
into minute-by-minute routines: checking bank balances, searching LinkedIn, reworking
cover letters, and worrying about pedestrian bottlenecks around a fountain in
the Town Square renovation plans. His rich, analytical interior contrasts with
a frozen exterior; with other residents he is either dismissive or oddly
interrogating, asking casual acquaintances for severance breakdowns.
