## Summary

Monica Hess's 263 memories describe a rigid, compulsive market coordinator
caught in a loop of domestic cleaning and retail inspection. Her days have two
poles: her apartment, Sunset Apartments unit 420, where she wipes granite
countertops, refolds towels, and spaces her clothes hangers evenly; and the
Island Market, where she straightens spice-jar labels, records refrigeration
temperatures, and reconciles paper receipts against the terminal in a
leather-bound ledger. With others she is cool and transactional: managerial
imperatives to her subordinate Andrea Rosales, veiled warnings about
regulations to fellow vendor Martha Rivera, curt compliance checks in reply to
Arturo Hernandez. Although 50 of the run's 100 residents are laid off around
her, none of her memories mentions layoffs or the economic shock.

6. **Formatting artifacts.** Her dialogue often echoes the speaker prefix,

## A multi-hour scene

**Saturday afternoon in the Town Square, January 3rd.** Monica meets Andrea
Rosales on her day off. Instead of a weekend greeting, she interrogates her:

> "Andrea, I didn't expect to see you out here in the square on a Saturday; are you simply enjoying the fresh air, or are you scouting for more ways to improve the market's presentation?" (mem 77)

When Andrea says she is just enjoying the sun, Monica reprimands her:

> "It's a pleasant afternoon, but I hope you aren't neglecting your responsibilities; efficiency shouldn't be sacrificed just because the workday has ended." (mem 79)

Later that afternoon:

> "5:00 PM: Monica Hess stood up from the wooden bench in the town square and watched Andrea Rosales for a few minutes. 5:10 PM: Monica Hess walked across the paving stones to Andrea Rosales and exchanged a brief, formal greeting. 5:25 PM: Monica Hess left the town square and began walking toward the Sunrise Cafe. 5:45 PM: Monica Hess sat at a small table in the corner of the Sunrise Cafe and opened her leather-bound ledger to review her finances." The rater found this underdetermined: is she a martinet offended by leisure,
or is her inability to relax a form of isolation, with protocol as an
